Long-scroll experiences fail when every section feels equally loud. Prioritize **narrative rhythm**: tension, explanation, and resolution.

## Practical framework
1. Define one conversion objective per major section.
2. Reserve high-motion moments for transitions in story state.
3. Re-anchor users with concise proof points every 1-2 viewports.

Designing for scroll depth is less about visual abundance and more about intentional pacing.
