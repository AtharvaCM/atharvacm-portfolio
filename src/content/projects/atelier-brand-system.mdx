## Challenge
The brand lacked a scalable digital system and struggled with inconsistent campaign execution.

## Approach
We defined a flexible editorial grid, reusable visual motifs, and tokenized component rules.

## Process
1. Brand audit and content inventory.
2. System prototyping with interactive references.
3. Documentation and handoff with governance guardrails.

## Result
The new system enabled faster launches while preserving a cohesive premium identity.
