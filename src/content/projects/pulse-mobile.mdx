## Challenge
Pulse had fragmented onboarding narratives across app stores, landing pages, and in-product setup screens.

## Approach
We aligned copy, interaction pacing, and visual hierarchy to keep user expectations consistent across channels.

## Process
- Unified conversion model and event map.
- Introduced reusable motion patterns for feature storytelling.
- Rebuilt onboarding checkpoints with clearer success states.

## Result
Retention and completion metrics improved while reducing experimentation overhead for the growth team.
