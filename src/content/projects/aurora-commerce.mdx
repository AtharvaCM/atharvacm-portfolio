## Challenge
Aurora needed to reposition from a template-heavy storefront to a premium digital brand while preserving performance and checkout clarity.

## Approach
We redesigned the information architecture around customer intent and built modular storytelling sections for campaign flexibility.

## Process
1. Conversion audit and heatmap review.
2. Visual direction with motion prototypes.
3. Component system implementation and iterative launch experiments.

## Result
The relaunch improved conversion quality and supported faster campaign publishing with reusable page blocks.
