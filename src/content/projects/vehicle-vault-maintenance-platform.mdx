## Overview

Vehicle Vault is an India-focused ownership platform for cars and two-wheelers: one place for what a vehicle has had done, what it is covered by, what it costs, and what is due next. Since the first release the product has grown from a maintenance log into a domain model with its own vocabulary, an alert engine, and a triage dashboard that opens on what needs attention rather than on a wall of charts.

## The ownership model

Most of the interesting work is in refusing to flatten things that behave differently. A **vehicle document** is a time-bounded record — insurance, warranty, registration, PUC, road tax — unified behind one service with per-kind adapters, so the three compliance kinds share a table while insurance and warranty keep theirs. A **maintenance record** is a service visit with line items, costs, and an odometer reading, which is why it is not a document. An **accessory** is a purchase with a fitment lifecycle, kept out of maintenance so it cannot corrupt the cost-per-kilometre figure that maintenance history exists to produce. Tyres are tracked by measurement rather than inferred, with the spare graded but excluded from the vehicle's mileage.

On top of that sit loans with amortization, prepayments, and foreclosure; claims linked to both a policy and the repair that settled them; and shared vehicle access with owner, editor, and viewer roles, invites, and ownership transfer — for the household where one person maintains everyone's vehicles.

## Knowing what is due

The alert engine is a daily cron that reads each vehicle's predicted odometer and raises typed alerts: maintenance due and overdue, reminders approaching their odometer, documents expiring, accessory warranties running out, tyres worn, aged, or unmeasured for too long, and service baselines that were never established. Each kind owns its own template and dedup key, so a notification row is upserted rather than duplicated, and delivery fans out to email and web push without either channel being able to break the record.

Baselines are the quiet part. A vehicle entering the vault has history nobody logged, so it carries a per-category baseline that is either a known figure or an explicit *unknown* — and an unmeasured tyre is treated as unknown rather than healthy, because that is a thing worth saying out loud.

## Document extraction and the catalog

Gemini-backed extraction reads service invoices, compliance papers, and loan documents into pre-filled drafts — straightening rotated photos, reading odometer and service date off an invoice, and surfacing failures instead of swallowing them. Vehicle entry is backed by a curated India catalog: staged imports reviewed in-app before publishing, curated aliases so messy real-world labels like "Old Swift ZXI" still resolve, and generation and year-aware offerings across more than thirty brands of cars and motorcycles.

## Delivery

The repo is a pnpm workspace with `apps/web`, `apps/api`, and shared contract and config packages, on conventional commits with semantic-release cutting versions and the changelog. CI runs typecheck, lint, Prettier, Vitest units, and Playwright smoke coverage of the core garage journey, plus a gate that fails the build when a public table ships without RLS enabled. The frontend deploys to Vercel; the API publishes as a GHCR image to a self-hosted Portainer stack and applies its own Prisma migrations on container start.
