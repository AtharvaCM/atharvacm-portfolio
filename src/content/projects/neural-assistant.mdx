## Challenge
Enterprise prospects struggled to map abstract AI features to practical use cases.

## Approach
We created scenario-driven sections that demonstrated outcomes by role and integrated lightweight interactive demos.

## Process
- Interviewed sales stakeholders for objection patterns.
- Designed problem-to-outcome storytelling template.
- Built high-performance transitions and selective interaction modules.

## Result
Buyers reached high-intent actions faster, and the team gained a scalable framework for future launch narratives.
